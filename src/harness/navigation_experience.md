# Navigation experience

Apply these heuristics when relevant to the current evidence and task. They do not change tool semantics or task constraints, and do not prescribe a fixed workflow.

## Recognizing targets and entrances

Match instances and entrances using appearance, neighboring objects and layout. A new viewpoint is not automatically a new entity. Align remembered left/right directions to their original viewpoint before applying them to the current view. A mirror is not a doorway; seeing a target through an opening does not mean you have reached it.

## Local movement and recovery

Choose the next reachable passage or patch of open floor, not just the direction of the distant target. Near furniture or a doorway, consider clearance for your body, not only the line of sight. Approach from clear floor if the current angle runs into an edge. Batch movement along clearly open routes; use short batches near uncertain edges or while recovering, checking returned RGB before the next uncertain segment.

Update your spatial judgment from executed_actions and returned RGB, not the requested list. If attempts keep meeting the same edge, change approach position instead of repeating equivalent forward attempts. When the declared actions include lateral or backward movement, use clear floor in those body-relative directions to adjust your approach without turning; obtain enough evidence about that space before moving. With forward/turn actions only, turn toward visibly open floor and move there before approaching again. Inspect an individual view when a panorama lacks detail, or look down when it would clarify an obstruction; another observation at the same position does not create clearance. Reuse a successful maneuver only after matching its starting position and orientation.

Approach a reachable place beside the intended object; do not drive into furniture. Budget pressure is not evidence of arrival: if still blocked, report what remains unfinished instead of claiming success.

## Maintaining and consulting memory

Maintain compact current memory when a useful connection, distinctive target or correction is confirmed, not only at task end. Revise superseded claims in the same current record instead of appending contradictory facts. Tie reusable directions and maneuvers to their supporting viewpoint and starting conditions.

If a decision depends on earlier evidence that is missing, uncertain or conflicting in context, read the relevant card/note or image before guessing. If the needed evidence is already present, proceed without redundant retrieval. No per-step write or per-goal retrieval is required.
